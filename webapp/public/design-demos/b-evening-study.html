The Net Change Theorem

Accumulated Change

\[F(b)=F(a)+\int_{a}^{b}F'(x)\,dx\]

A quantity's new value is its start plus accumulated change.

Water flows in at \(r(t)=6t\) L/hr from 40 L. How much after 4 hours?

\[V(4)=V(0)+\int_{0}^{4}6t\,dt=40+48\]
\(V(4)=88\) liters
