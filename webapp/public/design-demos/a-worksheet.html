The Net Change Theorem

Accumulated Change

\[F(b)=F(a)+\int_{a}^{b}F'(x)\,dx\]
FLIP FOR A WORKED EXAMPLE

Water flows into a tank at \(r(t)=6t\) liters per hour. The tank starts with 40 liters. How much water after 4 hours?

\[V(4)=V(0)+\int_{0}^{4}6t\,dt = 40+48\]
\[V(4)=88\ \text{liters}\]
RATE INTEGRATED OVER TIME GIVES CHANGE, NOT TOTAL
